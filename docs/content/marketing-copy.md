## Landing-page-length pitch

Most personal-finance and health-tracking apps ask you to hand your data to a company — an account, a server, a privacy policy explaining what they do with it. This one has none of that, because there's no company behind it to hand anything to.

Personal Life OS runs entirely on your own machine. Everything you enter — accounts and transactions, budgets, investments and their tax implications, health metrics, medications, financial and personal goals — lives in a single database file, encrypted with a passphrase only you know. It's self-hosted software, not a service: no sign-up, no cloud sync, no telemetry, nothing sent anywhere except one deliberate, manual mutual-fund price lookup you trigger yourself.

**What's inside:**
- **Finance** — accounts, transactions, budgets, debts and loan amortization, investments across every instrument type, capital-gains and tax planning, net worth over time, and an insights engine that flags what's worth your attention.
- **Health** — structured vitals, symptom logging, medication tracking with daily adherence.
- **Plans** — financial and non-financial goals, prioritized, with progress tracked automatically where it can be.
- **Dashboard** — a cross-module view of all of it at a glance.

It's free, open-source (AGPL-3.0), and yours to run, fork, or extend.

## Feature bullets (for a listing/README-style summary)

- 🔒 Encrypted at rest — SQLCipher-backed, unlockable only with your passphrase or a one-time recovery key
- 🏠 Self-hosted — runs on `127.0.0.1`, never exposed to your network or the internet
- 🚫 No account, no cloud, no telemetry — nothing about you or your data is ever sent anywhere
- 💰 Deep finance tracking — accounts, budgets, debts, investments, tax planning, net worth, and automated insights
- ❤️ Health tracking — vitals, symptoms, medication adherence
- 🎯 Goal tracking — financial and non-financial, prioritized, progress-tracked
